About this data

Ranking and locations: satop100courses.com (Golf Digest South Africa Top 100, 2026 list). Garden Route courses are shown as Western Cape (inferred, as George, Knysna, Mossel Bay, Plettenberg Bay and Herold's Bay are in that province). Map coordinates are approximate, town level (inferred). Played, wish list and league scores are saved to your account. Wish lists are private. Played courses and rounds are visible to members of your leagues.
